# CONNECT 10

**Connect 10** is a relaxing card puzzle game where the goal is to clear the board by selecting pairs of **adjacent cards** that either:

- **Sum to 10**, or  
- **Have the same value**

It’s simple, satisfying, and a great way to unwind.

---

## 🃏 Gameplay

- Click on two **neighboring cards** (horizontal or vertical only).
- If the cards **add up to 10**, or are **equal in value**, they are removed.
- Keep clearing the board until no valid moves remain.
- End the run to get money and buy upgrades.
- Complete missions to keep upgrading your score and money multipliers.

---
